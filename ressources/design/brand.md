# Brand & design reference (extracted from the Engage presentation and Instagram)

## Colors
| Role | Hex | Where it's used |
|---|---|---|
| Deep navy (primary) | #101B40 | titles, cards, nav pills |
| Off-white background | #F1F1F1 | slide backgrounds |
| Gold / amber (accent) | #E8A93A | numbers in content strategy, Instagram serif titles |
| Coral orange (accent) | #FF9A5C | "The Need" charts and 3% figure |
| Holographic cyan (accent) | #5CE1E6 | "The Opportunity"/"The Risk" charts |
| Instagram cream | ~#EFE6D6 | Instagram light posts |

## Typography used in the slides
- Montserrat Bold / Regular — titles (uppercase, bold + light mix: "PROBLEM **STATEMENT**")
- Open Sans — body text
- Lora — serif body in conclusion cards
- Instagram: elegant serif with gold italic accents ("Six minds. *One revolution.*")

## Visual style
- Light, airy slides with glassmorphism cards and floating AI icons (graduation cap, book, AI chip, brain) over a laptop/city background
- Dark cinematic slides for data (lecture hall, holographic teacher, shield) with neon line charts
- Pill-shaped navigation bar (HOME · ABOUT · CONTENT · OTHERS)
- Glowing earth + open book = "Knowledge Revolution" symbol
