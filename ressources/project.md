## One-sentence pitch
A deep learning, multimodal, generative AI project that brings 3D avatars of professors, thinkers and great minds — with their voice — to every learner, so they can learn wherever and whenever they want.

## The Challenge (official wording from the Engage presentation)
Allow every student, wherever they are, to ask questions and learn face to face from the best teachers and minds, in their own language.

## Essential Question
How can we make the best knowledge available to every learner, wherever and whenever they are?
