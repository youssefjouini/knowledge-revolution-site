# About the Project

## Overview
The Knowledge Revolution is a Deep Learning project developed by the FBAI's team (Future Builders of AI) at ESPRIT, following the Challenge Based Learning (CBL) approach, under the supervision of Mr. Safouane Jebali and Mrs. Wided Askri.

The goal is to turn education into an experience. Using deep learning, multimodal AI and generative AI, the project creates 3D avatars of professors, thinkers and great minds that can speak with a voice and answer students' questions. Students can learn whenever and wherever they want — the best teachers and minds come to them, at home or anywhere else.

## The problem, in the students' words
Six situations every student recognizes (slides 5–6):
1. Attended, but couldn't ask in front of everyone.
2. Missed the lecture.
3. Attended, still lost.
4. Tried an existing platform, still stuck.
5. Nobody answered my email.
6. Rewatched the recording twice — same confusion.

Real students' comments online end with the same cry: **"Is anyone there?"**

## Our vision (Conclusion & Perspectives)
- Real teachers' knowledge, available anytime.
- Every student gets an explanation that fits them.
- Students ask freely, without judgment.

## Our approach
- Deep learning & multimodal AI (voice, visuals, language)
- Generative AI to power the avatars' answers
- Interactive 3D avatars with realistic voice
- Responsible AI: AI-generated content is clearly disclosed (see Theme & Motivation → "The risk, our difference")
