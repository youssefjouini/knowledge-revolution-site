# AI & Methodology

## What we know so far
- Type: Deep Learning project, multimodal, Generative AI
- Output: 3D avatars of professors and thinkers that speak with a voice and answer students' questions
- Key features from the Challenge: face-to-face feeling, students can ask questions, answers in the student's own language
- Responsible AI principles: clear disclosure of AI-generated content (EU AI Act), awareness of voice-cloning risks, building trust (only 46% of people trust AI)
