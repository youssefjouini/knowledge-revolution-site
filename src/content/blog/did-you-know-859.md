**859.** That is the year the University of al-Qarawiyyin was founded in Fez, Morocco.

## A founder ahead of her time

The institution was founded by **Fatima al-Fihri**, a woman who used her inheritance to build a mosque and a place of learning for her community. More than eleven centuries later, it is still open.

## The oldest one still running

UNESCO and Guinness World Records recognize al-Qarawiyyin as the **oldest existing, continually operating educational institution in the world**. Over the centuries, it welcomed students and scholars in theology, law, grammar, mathematics, astronomy and medicine.

## Why it matters to us

Al-Qarawiyyin reminds us of something simple: education has always been about **bringing people and knowledge together**. In 859, that meant building a place where students could travel to meet the best teachers.

Our question in 2026 is the reverse one: *what if the best teachers could travel to every student instead?*

> Knowledge has always looked for new ways to reach people. We think its next home might be wherever you are.
