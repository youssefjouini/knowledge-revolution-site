Our team FBAI's presented the Engage phase of our Deep Learning project to our supervisors, Mr. Safouane Jebali and Mrs. Wided Askri.

## Where it starts: "Is anyone there?"

Every student knows these moments: attending a lecture but not daring to ask in front of everyone, missing the class, rewatching a recording twice and staying just as confused, sending an email that nobody answers. Online, students' comments all end with the same question: *is anyone there?*

## The numbers behind the need

The world will be missing 44 million teachers by 2030, 272 million children are still out of school (UNESCO), and only 3% of learners finish an online course (MIT/Harvard, Science 2019). At the same time, 95% of students already use AI (HEPI 2026).

## Our Engage phase

- **Big Idea:** Knowledge Revolution
- **Essential Question:** How can we make the best knowledge available to every learner, wherever and whenever they are?
- **Challenge:** Allow every student, wherever they are, to ask questions and learn face to face from the best teachers and minds, in their own language.

## Responsible by design

A voice can be cloned from 3 seconds of audio, and only 46% of people trust AI. Since August 2026, the EU AI Act requires AI-generated content to be disclosed. Our project will be transparent about its AI-generated avatars and voices.
