It's 2 a.m. You can't sleep. A question keeps turning in your head:

> *"Can we be free without knowledge?"*

Your teachers are asleep. Your friends too. A search engine gives you ten thousand links — but not a conversation.

## Now imagine…

Imagine you could ask the question to a philosopher, face to face. Not a list of quotes: a real answer, in your language, that starts from *your* question.

> *"Great question. Let's start with what you mean by 'free'…"*

That is how Socrates taught: not by giving lectures, but by **asking questions back** until his students found the answer themselves. That is the kind of learning we want to bring to every student.

## Your turn

This is where you come in. We want to build the avatars **with** the people who will use them.

- **Which great mind would you like to ask a question to?**
- **And what would you ask them?**
