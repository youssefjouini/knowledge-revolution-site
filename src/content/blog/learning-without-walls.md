For centuries, learning has had walls: the walls of the classroom, the walls of the timetable, the walls of language. If you were not in the room at the right time, you missed it. If you did not dare to raise your hand, your question stayed unanswered.

**The Knowledge Revolution** starts from a simple idea: what if the greatest minds came to you?

## 1. The best teachers, available anytime

Real teachers' knowledge should not disappear when the lecture ends. We want every student to be able to come back to it — at 8 a.m. or at 2 a.m., at home or on the bus.

## 2. Face to face, with a voice

Reading a transcript is not the same as being taught. Our project uses deep learning, multimodal AI and generative AI to create **3D avatars of professors, thinkers and great minds** that speak with a voice and answer students' questions.

## 3. In your own language

A great explanation is only great if you understand it. The Challenge we set ourselves is clear: every student should be able to learn *in their own language*.

## 4. Ask freely, without judgment

Many students never ask the question they have in mind because they are afraid of looking lost in front of everyone. An avatar never sighs, never judges, and is always happy to explain one more time.

## Responsible by design

Because the avatars and voices are generated by AI, we will always say so clearly. Transparency is not an option for us: it is part of the project.

> Learning without walls is not about replacing teachers. It is about making sure no student is ever left alone with a question.
