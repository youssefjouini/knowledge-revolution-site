Welcome to our project journal! Every week, we will share where we are, what we learned and what comes next.

## Where we are

Our project follows the Challenge Based Learning framework: **Engage → Investigate → Act**. This first week was all about Engage.

## Done this week

- ✅ **Picked our Big Idea:** The Knowledge Revolution
- ✅ **Wrote our Essential Question and our Challenge**
- ✅ **Formed the team:** six minds, one revolution — FBAI's, Future Builders of AI

## In progress

- 🔍 **Researching AI tutors and 3D avatars** — what already exists, what works, and what is still missing for students

## Coming next

- 🧪 **A first avatar prototype** — our "Avatar v0.1" is still a sketch on paper, but not for long

## What we learned

The hardest part was not finding an idea — it was choosing *one*. We kept coming back to the same moments that every student knows: the question you did not dare to ask, the lecture you missed, the email nobody answered. That is where our project comes from.

See you next week!
