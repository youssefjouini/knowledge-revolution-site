## Agenda

- Brainstorm ideas for our Deep Learning CBL project
- Choose our Big Idea
- Draft our Essential Question and our Challenge

## Discussion

We shared the problems we all live as students: not daring to ask in front of everyone, missing a lecture, rewatching a recording and staying just as confused, emails that nobody answers.

## Decisions

- **Big Idea:** The Knowledge Revolution
- **Essential Question:** How can we make the best knowledge available to every learner, wherever and whenever they are?
- **Challenge:** Allow every student, wherever they are, to ask questions and learn face to face from the best teachers and minds, in their own language.
